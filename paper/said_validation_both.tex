\section{Validation}
\label{sec:validation}

\noindent\textit{Validation status. Preliminary manual reading found the
generated Sinhala coherent in both datasets, but these observations are
informal and systematic evaluation is pending. Human-study sizes below are
targets; automatic figures are measured.}

\paragraph{Native-speaker quality.}
Two native Sinhala speakers will independently assess 600 texts: 450 from
Sinhala-HAT (150 per generator, 50 per domain) and 150 machine spans from
Sinhala-ABD (50 per generator). Blinded to the generator, they give yes/no
judgements of fluency and meaningfulness; ABD spans are judged in place and
carry a third judgement, whether the span preserves the facts it replaced. We
report mean yes rates per generator and inter-assessor agreement.

\paragraph{Human detection baseline.}
Ten native Sinhala speakers will each classify 90 Sinhala-HAT texts as human- or
AI-written (45 per class, 30 per domain) and mark the machine sentences in 10
Sinhala-ABD documents. True labels are hidden, and no participant sees both
members of a pair. We report HAT accuracy overall and by domain against a 50\%
chance rate, and ABD sentence-level accuracy against the 63.6\% majority-class
rate with the proportion of boundaries identified exactly. These are
initial-study sizes and do not guarantee statistical power. For comparison,
annotators reached 0.770 accuracy on Reddit in M4 \citep{wang2024m4}.

\paragraph{Automatic validation.}
Both datasets apply automatic checks before a record is accepted, and no output
is edited by hand. Sinhala-HAT requires output within $\pm$10\% of the paired
human word count and free of preamble and Markdown; failures are regenerated
twice, then the source is discarded and replaced. Sinhala-ABD applies seven
checks, adding exact sentence count, Sinhala-script and near-copy tests, and
unchanged numeric tokens, since its labels are only correct if the span is
usable in place of what it replaced. Across 5{,}109 rejected ABD generations,
length accounted for 53.7\% of check failures, sentence count 23.9\%, and
number mutation 16.3\%; 76.8\% of accepted documents passed on the first
attempt. Neither length tolerance was relaxed: generators under-write Sinhala,
and shorter machine text would let length substitute for authorship
\citep{doughman2024limitations}.
